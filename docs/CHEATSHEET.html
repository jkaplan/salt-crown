What matters most

Composition is the strategy surface. Mutations create variance, officers create reliability, and warband size creates economic pressure.

Mutants

Cheap, fragile, and mutation-driven. They are the volume play.

Low cost Random growth No bionics

True Kin

Expensive, reliable, and bionic-friendly. They are the quality play.

Stable stats Officer-led Bionics

Machines

Ruins-only, much stronger, and permanently dangerous to own.

Very strong Decay Not purchasable

Officer ranks

Officers are the named True Kin tier. They are individually recruited and each leads one squad.

Rank Cost Bonus Morale boost Use
SerjeantShouts until it works. 85 +18% squad damage +5% hold Offense amplification. Best on damage-heavy squads.

OutriderArrives before the order does. 80 +25% move speed, +5% evade +4% morale Fast support. Pairs with mobility or flanking plans.

Morale boost means the squad breaks later. A Warden-led squad holds much longer than an unled one.

Combat math

The sim is deterministic and tick-based. Combat resolves from the same snapshot each tick, so ordering bias does not matter.

Hit chance attacker_accuracy - defender_evade

Clamped between 5% and 95%.

Damage dealt max(1, attacker_damage - defender_armor)

Armour is flat reduction, not percentage.

Attack interval 20 Hz / attackInterval_ticks

A 10 tick interval means 2 attacks per second.

Discipline

  • Mutants: 35% effectiveness when broken.
  • True Kin: 78% effectiveness when broken.
  • Machines: 100%; they never degrade.

Range thresholds

  • ≤40 counts as melee and can trigger Quills reflections.
  • >40 counts as ranged and avoids melee-only counterplay.

Composition logic

The game is not about counter triangles. It is about building a warband that can survive its own costs and still scale.

Five mutant kinds

  • Chaff — mass and nothing else.
  • Sludge-Waller — raw HP.
  • Thornback — flat armour.
  • Stiltwalker — evasion.
  • Spitter — range.

Why officers matter

True Kin officers give guaranteed bonuses and better morale. Mutations are random; officers are reliable. That reliability is worth a lot when salvage is tight.

Economy

Salvage funds recruits and upgrades. Water and machine upkeep exist later in the design, but salvage is the current balancing lens.

Battle reward

~60 + encounter_index × 34

Scavenge

Stable, around 100 salvage expected value.

Delve

High variance, around 220 salvage expected value, plus better finds.

Firstborn

A Firstborn is a mutant named tier unit, promoted from a survivor rather than hired.

When a Firstborn leads a mutant squad, that squad’s mutations leak out to the whole squad at half level, minimum 1. This is how mutant growth becomes army-wide scaling.

Seeds & determinism

The same seed yields the same commander options, encounters, and battle outcomes for identical choices.

  • Same 15 encounters in the same order.
  • Same 3 commander options on leader select.
  • Same enemy warbands and mutation rolls.
  • Identical battle results if you make the same decisions.
